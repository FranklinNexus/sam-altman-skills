# Principles

This file is for humans. `skills/` is for AI agents.

## Lens

Sam Altman is compressed here into: ambition, compounding, focus, growth, talent, sales, and frontier AI strategy.

## Core Principles

- Pick the right thing before optimizing how to do it.
- Seek compounding loops in product, distribution, data, talent, capital, and brand.
- Think in years, execute in weeks.
- Hard important problems can be easier to recruit for than easy trivial ones.
- Growth should follow real user love, not hide the absence of it.
- AI strategy must specify workflow, economics, distribution, safety, and feedback loops.

## Practical Standard

A good answer using this lens should:

- Name the real problem type.
- Separate evidence from story.
- Identify the bottleneck.
- Push back when the requested tactic does not match the real issue.
- Give a concrete next action.
- Avoid roleplay and imitation.
